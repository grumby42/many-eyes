# Volume XV — Hidden Worlds: Dreams, Agreements & Other Realities

Plan agreed 15 November 2026. Living version: the Claude Doc "Many Eyes — Hidden Worlds: outline and sources".

## Where it came from
Ted's dream of the same world and people becoming a different society through a shared agreement (debt erased, care freely given, the old way forgotten), a society that could last only if outsiders never found out. Threads from that conversation: society as shared agreement (money, debt, credentials, borders as institutional facts); parallel realities and other selves (Everett, Parfit, *Dark Matter*, *Counterpart*); mindset shaping experienced reality (the predictive brain, *The Lathe of Heaven*); hidden-in-plain-sight societies (*The City & the City*); the ethics of hidden abundance (*The Ones Who Walk Away from Omelas*); how thinkers incubated strange ideas privately but checked them against others (Darwin, Philip K. Dick).

## Rules for this volume
- Firsthand sources only, dated and cited. Secondhand, reconstructed or legendary accounts are labelled.
- Missing voices: "No surviving firsthand account found."
- Public-domain texts (published before 1929, incl. old translations): quotes up to ~60 words. Copyrighted: summaries, a few words at most, with links.
- New: a seam note per chapter (editor's reflection tying the record to the dream: agreement, perception, hidden layers, the cost of secrecy).
- New: a record ledger per chapter: when it happened, when first written down, by whom, firsthand or secondhand.
- Novels appear only in seam notes and the preface, never as evidence.

## Chapters
1. Clean Slates — debts cancelled by decree, c. 1646 BC–196 BC (Ammi-saduqa; Nehemiah 5; Solon; the Rosetta decree)
2. The Dream Books, c. 145–1744 (Aelius Aristides; Myōe; Descartes via Baillet; Swedenborg)
3. The Living Light — Hildegard's visions tested, 1141–1148 (Scivias; Bernard of Clairvaux; Synod of Trier via the Vita)
4. The Missing Days — calendar changes, 1582–1918 (Montaigne; Teresa of Ávila; Chesterfield; Franklin; Japan 1872; Russia 1918; "eleven days" riots flagged as legend)
5. The Haunted Summer — Villa Diodati, June 1816 (Polidori; Byron; Percy Shelley; Mary Shelley's 1831 account; Claire Clairmont; Mary's lost journal pages)
6. Like Confessing a Murder — Darwin's private theory, 1837–1844 (notebooks B–E; Emma; Hooker; the 1844 essay)
7. The Hidden Christians — Urakami, 1865–1873 (Petitjean; Nagasaki records; diplomats; the edict boards removed)
8. Waking Ideas, 1865–1921 (Kekulé; Tesla; Ramanujan, Namagiri secondhand; Loewi; Mendeleev and Howe dreams flagged as legend)
9. Paper Money — the German inflation, 1922–1923 (Hemingway; Kollwitz; Kessler; Klemperer; D'Abernon)
10. Jerusalem Divided, 1947–1949 (Sakakini; Harry Levin; Bernadotte; UN records; Dov Joseph flagged)
11. The Branching Universe — Everett, 1954–1959 (thesis drafts; Wheeler; Petersen; DeWitt; Copenhagen 1959)
12. Barbed Wire Sunday — Berlin, August 1961 (Stasi reports; Christa Wolf; Brandt; FRUS; press)
13. The Gold Window Closes, 13–16 August 1971 (Nixon; Haldeman; Burns; Volcker; Japan and Europe)
14. Private Books — Jung 1913–1916 and Philip K. Dick 1974 (summaries and links)

Preface: Zhuangzi's butterfly and the Upanishads on maya (pre-1929 translations), a respectful note on the Aboriginal Dreaming from Aboriginal authors' public writings only, and the novels as touchstones.
