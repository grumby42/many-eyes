# Queued volumes XVI and XVII (requested 15 Nov 2026)

Order agreed: XIV *The Assay*, then X, XI, XII, then XVI and XVII, then second editions.

## XVI — Voices and Visions
Djinn, spirits, angels and demons, and messages taken as from God that started movements, ended states, or raised or removed leaders. Each experience is recorded as witnesses and opponents described it; no verdict on whether it was supernatural; every faith treated alike.
Candidates: Constantine's sign 312 · Joan of Arc 1429–31 (moved here from XI) · Loudun 1632–34 · Sabbatai Zevi 1665–66 · Handsome Lake 1799 · Joseph Smith's First Vision (accounts 1832–42) · Hong Xiuquan and the Taiping 1837–53 · the Báb 1844 · Nongqawuse 1856–57 · the Mahdi 1881–85 · Wovoka and the Ghost Dance 1889–90 · Fátima 1917 · djinn in Ottoman and Arab records (Evliya Çelebi).

## XVII — Below and Beyond
The depths and the heights: submarines, caves, mines, underground cities, the deep sea and space. Threads to III (poles), VII (rockets), II (Apollo 11).
Candidates: Bushnell's Turtle 1776 · Mammoth Cave and Stephen Bishop 1838 · H. L. Hunley 1864 · HMS Challenger 1872–76 · Courrières 1906 · Beebe's bathysphere 1934 · Lascaux 1940 · Nautilus under the Pole 1958 · Trieste 1960 · Vostok 1 1961 · Apollo 13 1970 · Cappadocia's underground cities.
