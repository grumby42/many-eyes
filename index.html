A synoptic reader

Many Eyes

Each chapter takes one event and lays real diaries, letters, notebooks, minutes and dated records from many people side by side on a shared timeline: who saw what, where, and on which day. Every volume is a single offline HTML file; open it in any browser, or save it for reading without a connection.
